MOUR PRIVACY POLICY Effective Date: September 6, 2026 Last Updated: September 15, 2026

Mour App LLC (“MOUR,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we
collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use the MOUR platform.



1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum personal information necessary to operate the platform. This includes:

    ●​ Account information (name, email, phone number, and profile details)
    ●​ Precise GPS location data, collected solely to verify the location of photos and videos
    ●​ Job and transaction metadata
    ●​ Government-issued photo identification and selfie verification when required for legal compliance
    ●​ Device and technical information (IP address, device type, and app usage data) for security and
        functionality

Important: MOUR does not access, view, review, or store the content of private photos or videos. All
media is transferred directly between users via encrypted peer-to-peer connections.



2. Media Privacy Settings

Users may choose whether media is private or public:

    ●​ Private Media: Delivered directly to the Requester. MOUR does not store private media beyond
        any temporary period required for delivery.
    ●​ Public Media: May be stored in our database and retained only for the duration required by
        applicable law.



3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

    ●​ Operate and improve the platform
    ●​ Verify media locations
    ●​ Process payments and job transactions
    ●​ Enforce our Terms of Service and legal obligations
    ●​ Prevent fraud and illegal activity
    ●​ Comply with record-keeping requirements

4. Data Minimization

We are committed to data minimization. We collect and retain only the information that is strictly
necessary to provide and secure the platform. We do not collect or store unnecessary personal data.



5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We only share information with:

    ●​ Third-party service providers that assist us in operating the platform (such as payment processors,
        cloud hosting providers, and our SMS delivery provider), all of whom are contractually obligated
        to protect your data and process it only to provide those services
    ●​ Law enforcement or regulatory authorities when required by law or valid legal process
    ●​ Other users only to the extent necessary to facilitate a job

Mobile phone numbers and SMS messaging consent (opt-in) data are not shared, sold, rented, or provided
to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Service providers that send
OTP texts on our behalf may process phone numbers solely to deliver those messages.



6. Data Retention

    ●​ Private Media: Not retained beyond temporary delivery requirements.
    ●​ Public Media: Retained only as long as required by applicable law.
    ●​ Personal Information: Upon account deletion, we will delete your personal information within thirty
        (30) days, except for records we are legally required to retain (including media consent and
        verification records).



7. International Users

MOUR primarily operates in the United States but accepts users from outside the United States. By using
the platform, you consent to the transfer of your information to and processing in the United States.



8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

    ●​ Access the personal information we hold about you
    ●​ Request correction or deletion of your data
    ●​ Request a copy of your personal information (data portability)
    ●​ Object to or restrict certain processing of your data
    ●​ Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, contact us at admin@mourapp.com.

9. Children’s Privacy

The MOUR platform is strictly limited to individuals who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly
collect, use, or disclose personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If we become aware that
we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.



10. Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your
information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. No security system is perfect, and we cannot
guarantee absolute security.



11. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will notify affected users and
relevant authorities as required by applicable law.



12. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the platform, remember preferences, and
analyze usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.



13. SMS / Text Messaging (OTP)

MOUR may send transactional one-time passcode (OTP) SMS text messages to the mobile
number you provide, solely for account authentication, login, or signup verification. These
messages are transactional OTPs only — not marketing or promotional texts.

    ●​ Message frequency: on-demand / as needed when you request a verification code (not
        a recurring campaign)
    ●​ Message and data rates may apply
    ●​ Reply HELP for help; reply STOP to opt out of further SMS from this program
    ●​ Consent to receive OTP SMS is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services
    ●​ Required non-sharing statement: We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number
        or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
    ●​ No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional
        purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support of the OTP service (such as our SMS
        delivery provider) is permitted only to deliver those authentication messages. Text messaging
        originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties for their marketing
        or promotional purposes.

15. Contact Us
